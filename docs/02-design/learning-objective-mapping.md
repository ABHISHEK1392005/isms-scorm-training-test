# Learning Objective Mapping

## Purpose

This document maps each course learning objective to the assessment questions designed to measure it.

The mapping ensures that every mission objective is assessed through relevant scenario-based questions and that the question bank remains aligned with the course design.

## Mission 1 — Protect the Information

### M1-O1
Classify workplace information and recognize confidentiality, integrity, and availability requirements.

| Question ID | Assessment Focus | Interaction Type |
|---|---|---|
| M1-Q01 | Recognize an integrity risk in a workplace document | Multiple Choice |
| M1-Q02 | Apply the course training policy to classify sensitive workplace information | Classification |
| M1-Q03 | Recognize an availability requirement in a workplace scenario | Scenario Decision |
| M1-Q04 | Apply the course training policy to classify internal workplace information | Classification |

## Mission 2 — Spot the Phishing Attack

## Alignment Notes

- Each mission contains one primary learning objective.
- Each learning objective is assessed by four questions.
- Each question is worth 5 points.
- Each mission therefore has a maximum score of 20 points.
- The complete course has a maximum score of 100 points.
- M5 is the fifth and final assessed stage of the course.
- No separate sixth assessment stage is used.
- M5 integrates concepts from the previous missions rather than introducing an unrelated topic.
- The question bank uses multiple interaction types, including multiple-choice, multi-select, classification, branching decisions, scenario decisions, and ordered responses.
- Classification terminology is based on the course training policy and is not presented as mandatory terminology defined by ISO/IEC 27001.
- The mapping is intended to support traceability between learning objectives, assessment questions, scoring, and the final course result.
